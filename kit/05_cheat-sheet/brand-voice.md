# FLOG: brand + voice cheat sheet

**half frog. half log. every fee buys $FLOG and locks it for 70 years.**

## who he is
- **flog** was a normal frog. he bought a coin, sat on a log to watch the chart, and kept sitting. he turned into wood. doctors call it **lignification**.
- **the look:** toy-like 3d frog head (big black eyes, wavy mouth, a leaf on top) on an upright bark-log body with a tree ring for a belly. green legs. always just standing there.
- **what's real (say it straight, never round it up):** a bot (the sawmill) claims the pump.fun creator fees every 5 minutes. once about 2 SOL stacks up (or after 24h with at least 1 SOL) it buys $FLOG and locks it in a Streamflow token lock nobody can cancel, for 70 years. each lock costs about 0.168 SOL (mostly account rent) plus 0.5% of the tokens. every claim, buy and lock is in the flog log. the dev locked 66,728,855 $FLOG (6.67% of supply) on Streamflow until 2093-11-23.

## voice
- lowercase. short sentences. deadpan. dumb but sincere.
- never explain the joke. the real mechanic is the punchline: say it plainly.
- never hype: no "moon", "100x", "gem", "send it", no price talk, no promises, no "your money comes back".
- flog doesn't shout. the one exception is an impact-font meme.
- end on the flat line, not on an exclamation mark.

running jokes: flog fears fire (never says it). the beaver is not your friend. flog can't sell, swim (he floats), clap, tap a phone, or leave. found photos: he keeps turning up standing still in places he shouldn't be.

## make a meme in 3 steps
1. pick a background from `02_backgrounds` (clean or cursed, square or wide).
2. drop a cutout from `01_cutouts` on it. put `extra/parts/ground_shadow.png` under his feet. keep him small and still, like a found photo.
3. caption it: white-bar top caption or impact (see `03_caption-templates`). for the found-photo look add `overlay_date-stamp` or `overlay_vhs-*`.
