short

flog held so long he turned into wood. every creator fee buys $FLOG and locks it on Streamflow for 70 years. every log is in the flog log.

long

flog was a normal frog. he bought a coin, sat on a log to watch the chart, and kept sitting. he turned into wood. half frog. half log.

$FLOG runs a sawmill: a bot that claims the coin's pump.fun creator fees every 5 minutes into a public fee wallet. once about 2 SOL stacks up (or after 24 hours with at least 1 SOL), it buys $FLOG on the market and locks it in a non-cancelable Streamflow token lock for 70 years. each lock costs about 0.168 SOL (mostly account rent) plus 0.5% of the locked tokens. nobody can cancel, move or shorten a lock.

every claim, buy and lock is written down in the flog log, with links to the transactions and the lock contracts. don't trust the frog. check the log.

the dev locked 66,728,855 $FLOG (6.67% of supply) on Streamflow until 2093-11-23.

logs don't sell.
